A Fragile World Above the Trees

Summit Lake Park sits in the alpine tundra zone of the Mount Evans Wilderness, one of the most ecologically sensitive landscapes in North America. Above treeline, plants and animals have adapted over millennia to extreme cold, intense UV radiation, and a brutally short growing season.

Today, this ecosystem faces compounding threats: rising temperatures driven by climate change, increasing recreational pressure from nearby Denver, invasive species encroachment, and degradation of water quality in the lake itself.

This proposal outlines a science-based, community-supported conservation plan to ensure that Summit Lake Park remains a thriving alpine ecosystem for generations to come.

"The alpine tundra is not a wasteland — it is a living mosaic of extraordinary resilience, and it demands our most careful stewardship."
— Colorado State University Alpine Research Station
